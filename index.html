Our Cafe

A warm welcome, every time you visit

Tucked away in Vine Mews, Cafe Mews has been a gathering place for Evesham families for years — the kind of spot where the kettle is always on and the tables are always spotless. We bake fresh every morning, cook to order, and treat every guest like a regular.

From little ones tucking into their first hot chocolate to grandparents settling in for a proper afternoon tea, our team looks after every table with the same warmth and care. Good food, kept simple and done properly — that's the whole idea.
